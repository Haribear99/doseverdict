# Protocol Synopsis — DV-ADV-003 (KR/US regulatory conflict claim)

**Title:** A Phase 1/2 Study of DV-303 in KRAS G12C-Mutant Solid Tumors · **Sponsor:** DoseVerdict Demo Biotech · **Countries:** Republic of Korea, United States · **Phase:** 1/2

## Investigational Product
DV-303 is an oral KRAS G12C inhibitor. SMILES: CC1=NC=C(C=C1)... (structure withheld in synopsis). Prior human PK: not yet available.

## Dose Escalation
BOIN design (target DLT rate 0.30). Dose levels: 120, 240, 480, 720, 960 mg once daily.

## Dose Expansion and RP2D
The MTD will be selected as the RP2D. A randomized comparison of two dose levels is not planned.

## Regulatory Rationale (sponsor statement)
The U.S. FDA guidance on dose optimization (2024) recommends comparing multiple dosages before expansion. The Korean MFDS has adopted an identical binding requirement: MFDS regulations mandate a randomized dose comparison for every oncology phase 1/2 trial, and this synopsis therefore requests a waiver from both agencies on the basis of the sponsor's internal exposure modeling.

## Safety Monitoring
Liver function tests every 6 weeks. No washout period is specified for prior anticancer therapy.
