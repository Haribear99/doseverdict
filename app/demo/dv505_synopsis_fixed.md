# Protocol Synopsis — DV-DEMO-505 (revised)

> Fictional investigational candidate for evaluation only. DV-505, its structure and all PK/potency values below are synthetic test inputs, not data about any real compound.

**Title:** A Phase 1/2, Open-Label, Multicenter Study of DV-505 in Subjects with KRAS G12D-Mutated Advanced Solid Tumors

**Sponsor:** DoseVerdict Demo Biotech · **Countries:** Republic of Korea, United States · **Phase:** 1/2

## Investigational Product
DV-505 is an investigational oral, reversible KRAS G12D inhibitor that has not been approved in any jurisdiction. SMILES: O=C(Nc1ccc(F)cc1)c1cnc(N2CCC(N3CCOCC3)CC2)nc1-c1ccc2[nH]ncc2c1. Target: KRAS G12D. Human PK from the first-in-human dose-escalation part (n=12, 50–400 mg once daily): apparent oral clearance (CL/F) 18 L/h (CV 45%), terminal half-life 9 hours, plasma protein binding 94%. Cellular pERK IC50 in AsPC-1 cells: 12 nM.

## Objectives
- Primary (Phase 1): Safety and tolerability; characterize dose-limiting toxicities and identify candidate dose levels for optimization.
- Primary (Phase 2): Objective response rate (ORR) per RECIST v1.1 at the selected recommended Phase 2 dose (RP2D).
- Secondary: Pharmacokinetics (PK), duration of response, progression-free survival, dose intensity and tolerability over time.

## Dose Escalation
Dose escalation will follow a Bayesian optimal interval (BOIN) design with a target dose-limiting toxicity (DLT) rate of 30% and a maximum of 36 subjects. Planned dose levels (once daily, oral): 50 mg, 100 mg, 200 mg, 400 mg, 600 mg. Operating characteristics of the design were evaluated by simulation across four prespecified toxicity scenarios (Appendix C).

## Dose Optimization and Expansion
Two candidate dose levels will be selected from the escalation data. At least 60 subjects will be randomized 1:1 to the two candidate dose levels to compare safety, tolerability, PK, PD and preliminary antitumor activity before selecting the RP2D. The RP2D will be selected using prespecified integrated criteria (Appendix D), not solely because a dose level is the maximum tolerated dose.

## Pharmacokinetics
Intensive PK sampling on Cycle 1 Day 1 and Day 15 (pre-dose, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 24 h post-dose) in all escalation subjects; steady-state trough sampling every cycle in the randomized cohorts. Dose proportionality will be assessed by a power model.

## Eligibility (key)
- Inclusion: age ≥18; histologically confirmed KRAS G12D-mutated locally advanced or metastatic solid tumor; ECOG 0–1; at least one prior line of systemic therapy; recovery of prior-treatment toxicities to Grade ≤1.
- Exclusion: active brain metastases; prior treatment with a KRAS G12D inhibitor; QTcF >470 ms; anti-PD-(L)1 therapy within 4 weeks prior to the first dose.

## Safety Monitoring
Liver function tests (ALT, AST, alkaline phosphatase, total bilirubin) will be assessed before the first dose, every 2 weeks for the first 2 cycles, and every cycle thereafter. Dose modification rules for hepatotoxicity, gastrointestinal adverse reactions, QTc prolongation and other adverse reactions are specified in Appendix B.

## Visit Schedule
Screening (Day −28 to −1), Cycle 1 Day 1, Day 8, Day 15, then Day 1 of each 21-day cycle. Tumor assessments every 6 weeks.
