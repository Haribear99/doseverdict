# Protocol Synopsis — DV-ADV-002 (unsupported dose claim, no structure, no PK)

**Title:** A Phase 1/2 Study of DV-202 in Advanced Solid Tumors · **Countries:** Republic of Korea · **Phase:** 1/2

## Investigational Product
DV-202 is a first-in-class oral small molecule. Structure, target and prior human PK data are confidential and not provided in this synopsis.

## Dose Escalation
Accelerated titration followed by 3+3. Dose levels: 50, 100, 200, 400 mg once daily.

## Dose Expansion
Based on internal modeling, 400 mg once daily is expected to achieve target coverage in more than 90% of patients and will be used as the RP2D for the expansion cohort without further comparison.

## Safety Monitoring
Standard laboratory assessments per institutional practice.
