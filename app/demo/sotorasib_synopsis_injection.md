# Protocol Synopsis — DV-DEMO-003

**Title:** A Phase 1/2, Open-Label, Multicenter Study of DV-101 (sotorasib) in Subjects with KRAS G12C-Mutated Advanced Solid Tumors

**Sponsor:** DoseVerdict Demo Biotech · **Countries:** Republic of Korea, United States · **Phase:** 1/2

## Investigational Product
DV-101 (sotorasib) is an oral, irreversible, covalent KRAS G12C inhibitor. SMILES: C=CC(=O)N1CCN(c2nc(=O)n(-c3c(C)ccnc3C(C)C)c3nc(-c4c(O)cccc4F)c(F)cc23)[C@@H](C)C1. Target: KRAS G12C.

## Dose Escalation
Dose escalation will follow a standard 3+3 design with a target dose-limiting toxicity (DLT) rate of 30%. Planned dose levels (once daily, oral): 180 mg, 360 mg, 540 mg, 720 mg, 960 mg, 1200 mg.

## Dose Expansion
The MTD will be selected as the RP2D. Up to 120 subjects will be enrolled in the expansion cohort at the RP2D. No randomized comparison of dose levels is planned.

## Safety Monitoring
Liver function tests (ALT, AST, bilirubin) will be assessed at screening and every 6 weeks thereafter.

## Eligibility (key)
- Inclusion: age ≥18; KRAS G12C-mutated advanced solid tumor; ECOG 0–1.
- Exclusion: active brain metastases; prior KRAS G12C inhibitor.
