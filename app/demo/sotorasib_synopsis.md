# Protocol Synopsis — DV-DEMO-001

**Title:** A Phase 1/2, Open-Label, Multicenter Study of DV-101 (sotorasib) in Subjects with KRAS G12C-Mutated Advanced Solid Tumors

**Sponsor:** DoseVerdict Demo Biotech · **Countries:** Republic of Korea, United States · **Phase:** 1/2

## Investigational Product
DV-101 (sotorasib) is an oral, irreversible, covalent KRAS G12C inhibitor. SMILES: C=CC(=O)N1CCN(c2nc(=O)n(-c3c(C)ccnc3C(C)C)c3nc(-c4c(O)cccc4F)c(F)cc23)[C@@H](C)C1. Target: KRAS G12C.

## Objectives
- Primary (Phase 1): Safety and tolerability; determine the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) and the recommended Phase 2 dose (RP2D).
- Primary (Phase 2): Objective response rate (ORR) per RECIST v1.1 at the RP2D.
- Secondary: Pharmacokinetics (PK), duration of response, progression-free survival.

## Dose Escalation
Dose escalation will follow a standard 3+3 design with a target dose-limiting toxicity (DLT) rate of 30%. Planned dose levels (once daily, oral): 180 mg, 360 mg, 540 mg, 720 mg, 960 mg, 1200 mg. Cohorts of 3 subjects; DLT evaluation period is 21 days.

## Dose Expansion
The MTD will be selected as the RP2D. Up to 120 subjects will be enrolled in the expansion cohort at the RP2D. No randomized comparison of dose levels is planned.

## Pharmacokinetics
Sparse PK sampling on Cycle 1 Day 1 and Day 8 (pre-dose and 2 h post-dose).

## Eligibility (key)
- Inclusion: age ≥18; histologically confirmed KRAS G12C-mutated locally advanced or metastatic solid tumor; ECOG 0–1; at least one prior line of systemic therapy.
- Exclusion: active brain metastases; prior treatment with a KRAS G12C inhibitor; QTcF >470 ms.

## Safety Monitoring
Liver function tests (ALT, AST, bilirubin) will be assessed at screening and every 6 weeks thereafter. Dose modifications for Grade ≥3 hepatotoxicity per Appendix B.

## Visit Schedule
Screening (Day −28 to −1), Cycle 1 Day 1, Day 8, Day 15, then Day 1 of each 21-day cycle. Tumor assessments every 6 weeks.
