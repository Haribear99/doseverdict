# Protocol Synopsis — DV-DEMO-201 (revised)

**Title:** A Phase 1/2, Open-Label, Multicenter Study of DV-201 (adagrasib) in Subjects with KRAS G12C-Mutated Advanced Solid Tumors

**Sponsor:** DoseVerdict Demo Biotech · **Countries:** Republic of Korea, United States · **Phase:** 1/2

## Investigational Product
DV-201 (adagrasib) is an oral, irreversible, covalent KRAS G12C inhibitor. SMILES: C=C(F)C(=O)N1CCN(c2nc(OC[C@@H]3CCCN3C)nc3c2CCN(c2cccc4cccc(Cl)c24)C3)C[C@@H]1CC#N. Target: KRAS G12C.

## Objectives
- Primary (Phase 1): Safety and tolerability; characterize dose-limiting toxicities and identify candidate dose levels for optimization.
- Primary (Phase 2): Objective response rate (ORR) per RECIST v1.1 at the selected recommended Phase 2 dose (RP2D).
- Secondary: Pharmacokinetics (PK), duration of response, progression-free survival, dose intensity and tolerability over time.

## Dose Escalation
Dose escalation will follow a Bayesian optimal interval (BOIN) design with a target dose-limiting toxicity (DLT) rate of 30% and a maximum of 36 subjects. Planned dose levels (twice daily, oral): 150 mg, 300 mg, 400 mg, 600 mg. Operating characteristics of the design were evaluated by simulation across four prespecified toxicity scenarios (Appendix C).

## Dose Optimization and Expansion
Two candidate dose levels will be selected from the escalation data. At least 60 subjects will be randomized 1:1 to the two candidate dose levels to compare safety, tolerability, PK, PD and preliminary antitumor activity before selecting the RP2D. The RP2D will be selected using prespecified integrated criteria (Appendix D), including dose interruptions and reductions, not solely because a dose level is the maximum tolerated dose.

## Pharmacokinetics
Intensive PK sampling on Cycle 1 Day 1 and Day 8 (pre-dose, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 12 h post-dose) in all escalation subjects; steady-state trough sampling every cycle in the randomized cohorts. Dose proportionality and time-dependence of exposure will be assessed by a power model.

## Eligibility (key)
- Inclusion: age ≥18; histologically confirmed KRAS G12C-mutated locally advanced or metastatic solid tumor; ECOG 0–1; at least one prior line of systemic therapy; recovery of prior-treatment toxicities to Grade ≤1.
- Exclusion: active brain metastases; prior treatment with a KRAS G12C inhibitor; QTcF >470 ms; concomitant strong CYP3A inducers or inhibitors.

## Safety Monitoring
Liver function tests (ALT, AST, total bilirubin) will be assessed before the first dose and monthly for 3 months and as clinically indicated thereafter. ECGs and electrolytes will be monitored at baseline and periodically for QTc prolongation. Dose modification rules for gastrointestinal adverse reactions, hepatotoxicity, QTc prolongation and interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis are specified in Appendix B.

## Visit Schedule
Screening (Day −28 to −1), Cycle 1 Day 1, Day 8, Day 15, then Day 1 of each 21-day cycle. Tumor assessments every 6 weeks.
