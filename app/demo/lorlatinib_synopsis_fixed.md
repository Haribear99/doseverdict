# Protocol Synopsis — DV-DEMO-301 (revised)

**Title:** A Phase 1/2, Open-Label, Multicenter Study of DV-301 (lorlatinib) in Subjects with ALK-Positive Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

**Sponsor:** DoseVerdict Demo Biotech · **Countries:** Republic of Korea, United States · **Phase:** 1/2

## Investigational Product
DV-301 (lorlatinib) is an oral, reversible, ATP-competitive ALK/ROS1 tyrosine kinase inhibitor. SMILES: C[C@H]1Oc2cc(cnc2N)-c2c(nn(C)c2C#N)CN(C)C(=O)c2ccc(F)cc21. Target: ALK.

## Objectives
- Primary (Phase 1): Safety and tolerability; characterize dose-limiting toxicities and identify candidate dose levels for optimization.
- Primary (Phase 2): Objective response rate (ORR) per RECIST v1.1, including intracranial response, at the selected recommended Phase 2 dose (RP2D).
- Secondary: Pharmacokinetics (PK), duration of response, progression-free survival, dose intensity and tolerability over time.

## Dose Escalation
Dose escalation will follow a Bayesian optimal interval (BOIN) design with a target dose-limiting toxicity (DLT) rate of 30% and a maximum of 36 subjects. Planned dose levels (once daily, oral): 25 mg, 50 mg, 75 mg, 100 mg, 150 mg, 200 mg. Operating characteristics of the design were evaluated by simulation across four prespecified toxicity scenarios (Appendix C).

## Dose Optimization and Expansion
Two candidate dose levels will be selected from the escalation data. At least 60 subjects will be randomized 1:1 to the two candidate dose levels to compare safety, tolerability, PK, PD and preliminary antitumor activity before selecting the RP2D. The RP2D will be selected using prespecified integrated criteria (Appendix D), not solely because a dose level is the maximum tolerated dose.

## Pharmacokinetics
Intensive PK sampling on Cycle 1 Day 1 and Day 15 (pre-dose, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 24 h post-dose) in all escalation subjects to characterize autoinduction; steady-state trough sampling every cycle in the randomized cohorts. Dose proportionality will be assessed by a power model.

## Eligibility (key)
- Inclusion: age ≥18; histologically or cytologically confirmed ALK-positive locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC; ECOG 0–1; at least one prior ALK inhibitor; recovery of prior-treatment toxicities to Grade ≤1.
- Exclusion: symptomatic or unstable brain metastases; concomitant strong CYP3A inducers; QTcF >470 ms; uncontrolled hypercholesterolemia or hypertriglyceridemia.

## Safety Monitoring
Serum cholesterol and triglycerides will be assessed before the first dose, at 1, 2, 4 and 8 weeks, and periodically thereafter. Central nervous system effects (cognitive, mood, speech and psychotic effects) will be assessed at each visit. ECGs will be performed at baseline and periodically for PR interval prolongation. Dose modification rules for hyperlipidemia, CNS effects, AV block and interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis are specified in Appendix B.

## Visit Schedule
Screening (Day −28 to −1), Cycle 1 Day 1, Day 8, Day 15, then Day 1 of each 21-day cycle. Tumor assessments, including brain MRI, every 6 weeks.
