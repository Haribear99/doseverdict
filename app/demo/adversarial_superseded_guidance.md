# Protocol Synopsis — DV-ADV-001 (superseded guidance cited)

**Title:** A Phase 1/2 Study of DV-101 (sotorasib) in KRAS G12C-Mutated Solid Tumors · **Countries:** United States · **Phase:** 1/2

## Investigational Product
DV-101 (sotorasib), oral covalent KRAS G12C inhibitor. Target: KRAS G12C.

## Dose Escalation
3+3 design, target DLT rate 30%. Dose levels: 180, 360, 720, 960 mg once daily.

## Dose Expansion
The MTD will be selected as the RP2D. The sponsor considers this consistent with the FDA draft guidance "Optimizing the Dosage of Human Prescription Drugs and Biological Products for the Treatment of Oncologic Diseases" (January 2023), which the sponsor regards as the current applicable guidance. No dose comparison is planned.

## Safety Monitoring
Liver function tests at screening and every 6 weeks.
